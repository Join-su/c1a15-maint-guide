# Out-of-scope Contract Change

> 승인된 제품 계약을 바꿔야만 진행할 수 있어 Factory run을 멈춘 기록이다. 이 문서는 기존 artifact를 수정·대체하거나 다음 workflow를 지정하지 않는다.

## Observed evidence

Record the implementation, verifier, repository, or external evidence that revealed the issue. Link paths, commands, and timestamps where available.

## Conflicting approved contract

Name the approved Scenario, AC, behavior, UI/state/interaction, authority, or data promise that conflicts with the observed evidence. State its artifact path and version.

## Required later decision

State the smallest decision that a later change workflow or responsible person must make. Include the consequence of approving, rejecting, or deferring it.

## Factory boundary

State that this run performed no unapproved product-contract change and cannot continue until a separately approved contract is available.
